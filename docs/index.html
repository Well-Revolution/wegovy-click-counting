Click counting reference — every pen

From the Well Revolution Wegovy click counting guide. A full labelled dose is 37 clicks on the 0.5 mg pen and 75 clicks on the 3 mL pens.

Important: Using a Wegovy FlexTouch pen to deliver a dose other than its labelled strength is off-label and is not approved by the manufacturer or Medsafe. Click counts follow the Well Revolution Wegovy click counting guide: the 0.5 mg pen is 1.5 mL (148 clicks in total); the 1, 1.7 and 2.4 mg pens are 3 mL (300 clicks in total). Rounded click counts deliver slightly more or less than the nominal dose (for example, 8 clicks on a 2.4 mg pen delivers 0.26 mg rather than 0.25 mg). Manufacturer guidance is to discard a pen 6 weeks after first use — click counting many doses from one pen extends use well beyond this window; discuss storage and pen life with your prescriber. Figures use the pen price divided by full doses available from the pen's total clicks, assume 4 weekly doses per month, and don't allow for extra needles when taking more than 4 doses from one pen. Prices are from Chemist Warehouse NZ as at 21 July 2026 and may change. All dosing decisions must be made in consultation with your prescribing clinician.
